How it works

Up and running in 3 minutes.

No importing transactions. No linking your bank. Just enter your setup once and start knowing.

1
Enter your paycheck
Tell Budget Peace your income, pay cadence (bi-weekly, semi-monthly, or monthly), and first pay date.
2
Add your recurring bills
Add rent, subscriptions, insurance, loans — anything that hits your account regularly. Set the due date and frequency.
3
See exactly what's left
Every pay period shows you income minus all obligations. The real number — not your bank balance, not an average.

Everything you need to know.

YNAB and Mint are powerful, but they're built around transaction categories and monthly summaries. Budget Peace is built around your paycheck. You tell it your income, your bills, and when you get paid — and it tells you what's actually left each period. No categorizing every latte. No connecting your bank account.
No — and that's intentional. Budget Peace is a forward-looking planner, not a transaction tracker. You enter your expenses manually, which means you actually think about them. No stale data, no sync errors, no sharing your banking credentials with a third party.
A scenario is a complete copy of your budget with different numbers. Think of it as a what-if sandbox. You can have a "Current" scenario and a "New Job" scenario side by side — each with their own income, expenses, and pay dates — and compare them without touching your real budget.
